# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers align engineering execution with product and delivery goals. They guide team planning, capacity, and technical delivery while supporting developer growth and operational stability.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity, priorities, and work allocation
- Coordinate engineering dependencies and delivery sequencing
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on roadmap trade-offs
- Support technical planning, risk reduction, and delivery health
- Help remove blockers that affect sprint or milestone execution

### Goals
- Deliver predictable and sustainable engineering output
- Improve team focus, throughput, and quality
- Maintain alignment between technical strategy and business priorities

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and sprint reviews
- Dependency and escalation discussions with PMs and product leads
- Team-level delivery updates and capacity planning

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to prioritize work and unblock delivery
- Partners with Product Managers on scope, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Coordinates with Project Managers on milestones, risks, and cross-team dependencies

---

## QA Lead / Quality Manager

### Role Summary
The QA Lead defines quality expectations, test strategy, and release readiness. They ensure releases meet acceptance criteria and reduce the likelihood of user-facing defects.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release criteria
- Manage defect triage, prioritization, and verification workflows
- Coordinate testing across unit, integration, and end-to-end coverage
- Validate that user stories meet acceptance criteria and business outcomes
- Partner with Product and Engineering on risk-based testing and quality trade-offs

### Goals
- Improve release confidence and reduce production defects
- Ensure a consistent definition of quality across the project lifecycle
- Support faster, safer delivery decisions

### Typical Communication
- QA readiness reviews and defect triage sessions
- Release signoff and risk communication with stakeholders
- Test planning and validation feedback with engineering teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to verify implementation quality and test coverage
- Supports Product Managers by validating acceptance criteria and user outcomes
- Keeps Project Managers informed about blockers, quality risks, and release readiness

---

## Security / Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners assess risk, safeguard sensitive data, and ensure work follows internal policies and regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs for security and compliance implications
- Identify vulnerabilities, governance gaps, and required controls
- Define required review steps for sensitive features or data handling
- Escalate risk exposure and remediation priorities to the right owners
- Support secure release decisions and incident readiness

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Ensure work aligns with policy, legal, and operational requirements
- Protect customer trust and organizational accountability

### Typical Communication
- Design and risk review checkpoints
- Security exception handling and remediation updates
- Incident escalation and post-incident review coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product and Engineering on risk trade-offs and acceptable controls
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk tracking and mitigation plans
- Provides input to stakeholders and leadership on compliance implications and release decisions

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
This role connects the project to customer feedback, support experience, and product adoption outcomes. It helps translate operational insight into product and delivery priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer pain points from support and adoption data
- Capture feedback on usability, reliability, and service quality
- Help prioritize issues with customer impact or operational risk
- Coordinate communication between customer-facing teams and delivery teams
- Support readiness for rollout, support training, and issue monitoring

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption
- Connect product execution to real-world user experience
- Reduce friction between delivery teams and customer-facing operations

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and support issue triage
- Stakeholder updates on customer impact and service quality
- Product and release readiness discussions with operations teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds Product Managers with customer insights and urgency signals
- Shares operational risks and support patterns with Project Managers and Engineering Managers
- Helps Developers and QA understand real-world scenarios and high-impact regressions

---

## Data / Analytics Partner

### Role Summary
The Data and Analytics Partner defines how success will be measured and how outcomes are tracked over time. They support decisions with evidence and help the team learn from product usage and operational metrics.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Validate whether a project is delivering expected business or customer value
- Partner with Product and PM teams on outcome measurement
- Identify anomalies, trends, and signals that require action
- Support post-launch learning and continuous improvement reviews

### Goals
- Make decisions based on measurable evidence
- Improve confidence in product impact and release quality
- Enable learning loops across product and delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and metric analysis sessions
- Outcome tracking during roadmap and release reviews
- Retrospective discussions focused on measurable improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Product Managers define success criteria and evaluate business impact
- Provides evidence to Project Managers for milestone and release reporting
- Supports Developers and QA by identifying quality and usage signals that matter most

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded set of personas helps teams clarify ownership, escalation paths, and collaboration across delivery, quality, security, support, and analytics work.
