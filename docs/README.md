# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a structured lifecycle to turn project ideas into reliable delivery. The process begins with initiation, where teams validate the business problem, define stakeholders, document success metrics, and decide whether the effort should move forward into planning. Once approved, planning breaks the work into a backlog, release plan, milestones, dependencies, and a clear definition of done. This creates a foundation for sustainable execution and keeps teams focused on customer value, measurable outcomes, and realistic scope.

The operating model relies on clearly defined roles and consistent communication. Product managers define what should be built and how success will be measured, project managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and stakeholder alignment, and developers, QA, and other contributors execute and validate the work. Communication is intentionally regular: daily standups, weekly PM/Product syncs, milestone reviews, and stakeholder updates help teams identify blockers early and keep everyone aligned. Escalation follows a defined path from the team to the PM, Product Lead, and sponsor when issues become business-critical.

Quality and delivery discipline are central to OctoAcme’s way of working. Work is expected to be broken into small, reviewable increments, with pull requests tied to issues and acceptance criteria, automated testing and linting in CI, and security checks before merge. The documentation also emphasizes smoke testing before release, post-deployment verification, and rollback plans for production incidents. Risk management is treated as an ongoing activity, with a risk register, mitigation planning, and weekly review to surface and manage issues before they block progress.

The model closes the loop with release practices and continuous improvement. Each release follows a checklist for staging, verification, communication, and rollback readiness, while retrospectives capture what went well, what could improve, and the action items needed to move forward. By combining clear roles, repeatable workflows, and structured learning cycles, OctoAcme creates a practical project management system that supports strong execution and steady team improvement.
